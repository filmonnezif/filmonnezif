Filmon Nezif Abamecha

I am an undergraduate researcher in Electrical Engineering Technology at the Higher Colleges of Technology in Sharjah, UAE, graduating in August 2027.

I work on making robots and edge AI systems behave safely when conditions are not ideal: navigation stacks whose commands go stale, perception that has to fit on onboard hardware, and motion planning around people. I have worked with Dr Zakwan Skaf on aquaponics automation and edge AI, and with Dr Nur Siyam on Lexy, AI reading support for Arabic readers with dyslexia. I release code and data so my results can be reproduced.

 Does Better Pedestrian Prediction Make a Safer Robot? A Predictor, Uncertainty and Planner Benchmark on Real Crowds
Filmon Nezif
In preparation
code

Seven trajectory predictors, conformal uncertainty, space-time A* and three controllers, evaluated in 23 configurations on 120 ETH/UCY episodes. Space-time A* with MPC had 54% fewer contacts than A* with pure pursuit, and learned predictors were no safer than constant velocity.
