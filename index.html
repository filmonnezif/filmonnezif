About

I am an undergraduate researcher at the Higher Colleges of Technology in Sharjah, graduating in August 2027. I work on making robots and edge AI systems that behave safely when conditions are not ideal: navigation stacks whose commands go stale, perception that has to fit on onboard hardware, and motion planning around people.

I have done my research with Dr Zakwan Skaf (aquaponics automation and edge AI) and Dr Nur Siyam (Lexy, AI reading support for Arabic readers with dyslexia). I release code and data for my work so it can be reproduced.

Research interests: robot navigation safety under timing faults · efficient onboard perception · socially aware motion planning.

Selected projects

Crowd navigation among real pedestrians · repo
A MATLAB navigation stack with seven trajectory predictors, conformal uncertainty, space-time A* and pure pursuit, DWA and MPC control, evaluated in 23 configurations on 120 ETH/UCY episodes. Space-time A* with MPC had 54% fewer contacts than A* with pure pursuit, and learned predictors were no safer than constant velocity.
Nav2 timing-fault harness · repo
Fault injection and finite-trace monitoring for ROS 2 Jazzy / Nav2 command chains: does the chain stop by a deadline, stay stopped and recover? 80 trials over 8 configurations and 16 scenarios, with raw traces released.
Edge plant-disease detection
MobileNetV2 on a Raspberry Pi 4, compressed 14.5× to 612.5 KB with 1.8 ms latency at 1.8 W while keeping 94.0% accuracy.
WristEcho · repo
Smartwatch sound alerts for Deaf users. A DSP wake-up front end detected 13 points more than YAMNet at the same false-alarm rate and kept the classifier asleep 93% of the time.
Lexy · lexy-ai.com
Arabic-first AI reading support for children with dyslexia: text simplification, diacritisation and speech-based reading assessment. Team lead and lead engineer.
